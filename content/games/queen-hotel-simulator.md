A hotel management tycoon for Android. I programmed the core management loops, the modular room upgrade paths and the dynamic unlock flow.

## What I worked on

- Core tycoon loop: guests, rooms, income and upgrades.
- Save and progression system built on data serialization.
- Draw-call optimization that removed frame drops on low-end phones.

## Tools

Unity, C#, Android, Unity Profiler.
